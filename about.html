Meet the Trainer

Heide Maglott

👋 Hello Tampa Bay! I’m the founder of Cultured Canines of Tampa. I'm thrilled to bring positive, science-based dog training to this vibrant, pet-loving community.

With over 26 years of hands-on experience, I have made it my mission to help families build better relationships with their dogs through clear communication, compassion, and force-free methods only.

I proudly follow the guiding philosophy of No Pain. No Force. No Fear. That's not just a tagline—it’s a commitment I live by every single day. The science is clear: positive reinforcement training is effective, and it strengthens the bond in ways punishment-based methods simply cannot.

Why Only Positive Reinforcement?

Cultured Canines of Tampa exclusively uses positive reinforcement training because it builds trust, confidence, and clear communication between dogs and their families. By rewarding desired behaviors and creating positive learning experiences, dogs are encouraged to make good choices willingly and with confidence. This science-based approach creates lasting results while strengthening the bond between dogs and their people, making learning enjoyable, effective, and rooted in trust and emotional well-being.
