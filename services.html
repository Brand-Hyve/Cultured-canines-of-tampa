Dog Wellness & Sensory Enrichment

Sanctuary Sessions

A luxury, in-home emotional wellness program designed to restore balance to overstimulated, reactive, or anxious modern dogs.

Life in busy communities like Hyde Park, Davis Islands, Westchase, Carrollwood, Wesley Chapel, and Fishhawk can overwhelm dogs. This stress manifests as walk reactivity, pacing, restlessness, guest anxiety, or slipping obedience.

Sanctuary Sessions focus on your dog's emotional state:

  • Aromatherapy & Calming rituals to soothe the nervous system
  • Choice-based sensory enrichment to build self-confidence
  • Gentle tension-release and emotional regulation techniques
